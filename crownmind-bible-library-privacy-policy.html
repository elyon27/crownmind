Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026 · Last updated: 27 October 2026
Android package: net.crownmind.biblelibrary

This Privacy Policy explains how CrownMind Institute ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in the CrownMind Bible Library Android app ("the app") and when you contact us about it.

Your reading and reflections stay on your device. The app works offline, does not require an account, and does not send your notes, bookmarks, Bible searches, or reading preferences to us.

1. Information used on your device

The app stores your personal chapter notes, saved bookmarks, and reading or display preferences in local app storage, including Android WebView local storage. This information supports the app's reading and study features. Searches are processed against the bundled Bible content on your device.

Notes may contain personal or sensitive information, including religious reflections, depending on what you choose to write. We do not receive or access those notes. They are not published, shared with other users, or synchronized with a CrownMind Institute account.

2. Information we do not collect through the app

The app does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs. It does not collect or transmit your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, payment information, advertising identifier, reading history, notes, or bookmarks to us. It does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or shared-file access permissions. The Android app does not request Internet permission; its Bible and study content is bundled for offline use.

3. When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any attachments or other information you voluntarily provide. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot, and handle privacy requests. Please avoid including private notes or other sensitive information unless it is needed for your request.

Our email service provider processes support correspondence to deliver and store messages. We do not sell or rent your personal information. We disclose support information only as needed to provide support through our service providers, comply with applicable legal obligations, or protect rights and security.

4. External links and Google Play

Links to CrownMind Institute, Bible Gateway, or other online resources open outside the app in your browser or another appropriate application. Those services may process information such as your IP address, browser information, cookies, and information you submit, under their own privacy policies. A Bible passage link may include the selected passage reference in its address; your private notes and bookmarks are not attached.

Google Play and your device operating system may independently process installation, diagnostic, or usage information under their settings and privacy policies. Google Play may make app performance and distribution reports available to developers. These services do not give us access to your locally saved notes or bookmarks. See Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Storage, retention, and deletion

Your local app information remains on your device until you change or remove it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. We do not maintain a server copy or provide cloud synchronization. Android automatic app backup is disabled in the app configuration; device transfer behavior may also depend on your device manufacturer.

  • Remove a note: open its chapter, clear the note text, and save the empty note.
  • Remove all locally saved information: open Android Settings → Apps → CrownMind Bible Library → Storage (or Storage & cache) → Clear storage/Clear data. Menu names vary by device. This removes your saved notes, bookmarks, and preferences.
  • Uninstall: uninstalling normally removes local app data. We cannot recover data that exists only on your device.

There is no app account to delete. For support correspondence, we retain information only as long as necessary to resolve your request and meet applicable legal or security obligations. You may request deletion by email; where retention is required, we will explain the reason.

6. Security and your choices

The app relies on Android's app storage protections. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep its software updated. Anyone who can use your unlocked device may be able to read your saved notes. No device or storage method is completely secure.

You can use the reading features without saving notes or bookmarks. Contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of personal information you have sent us. We cannot remotely access, edit, or erase information stored only on your device.

7. Children's privacy

The app does not require users to provide personal information, regardless of age, and does not knowingly collect children's personal information through its offline features. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us by email, contact us so we can review and address it.

8. Scope and changes

This policy covers CrownMind Bible Library's Android app. Other CrownMind Institute website features and external services may have separate privacy practices. Viewing this policy online requires a request to the website host; the copy bundled inside the app can be read offline.

We may update this policy when the app or our practices change. We will publish the revised policy with an updated date and provide any additional notice or consent required for material changes.
