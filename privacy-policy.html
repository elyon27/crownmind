Your privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains how CrownMind Institute handles information in connection with the CrownMind website at crownmind.net, including its pages served through Netlify. For privacy questions or requests, contact leuneib01@gmail.com.

1. Scope of this policy

This policy covers this website’s reading resources, contact form, book links, and learning pages. Linked services—including CrownMind Community, CrownMind Articles, Tenio Theological University hosted separately, Cyber Altar, social media, and purchase platforms—may have different features and privacy practices. Consult the notice on the service you use. This policy does not describe additional data collection by a separately installed mobile app.

2. Information you provide

The contact form requests your name, email address, topic, and message. These details are submitted through Netlify Forms so CrownMind Institute can receive and respond to your inquiry. If you contact us by email or Messenger, the information you send is also processed by the relevant email or messaging provider.

Please provide only information needed for your inquiry. Do not include passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, or unnecessary sensitive information about yourself or others. Religious beliefs, health details, and private prayer concerns can be sensitive; they are not required to browse this website.

3. Website usage information and cookies

Pages on this website use Google Analytics to understand visits and improve content. Analytics can collect page views, interactions, referral information, browser and device details, approximate geographic information, and cookie identifiers. Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish visits. Its processing is described in Google’s information about partner sites and Google’s Privacy Policy.

The website’s existing analytics tags load automatically on pages where they are included; this website does not currently offer a cookie-consent preference panel. You can manage or delete cookies in your browser and, on supported browsers, use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Blocking cookies alone may not prevent all network requests or measurement.

Hosting and other service providers may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, requested URLs, timestamps, and browser details, to deliver content, maintain reliability, and prevent abuse.

4. Information stored on your device

A learning-module page uses local storage to remember whether you marked the module complete. This marker stays in that browser and is not submitted to CrownMind Institute by that feature. Clearing this site’s browser data removes the marker. Browser storage and downloaded files may remain on your device until you delete them.

5. How information is used and shared

Contact information is used to respond to questions, resource requests, invitations, or partnership inquiries. Usage information supports understanding readership and improving website content. Technical information supports website delivery, security, and troubleshooting.

Information is processed by providers involved in these activities, including Netlify for hosting and form handling, Google for analytics and email where used, and Meta if you choose Messenger. Providers may process information outside the Philippines under their own terms and privacy notices. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or necessary to address misuse or protect legal rights.

6. Book purchases and external links

Purchase buttons direct you to Gumroad. Checkout, billing, and payment information are handled on that platform; this website does not contain a payment-card entry form. Gumroad may make order information available to the seller to fulfill purchases and provide support. Review the privacy information shown at checkout before buying.

Following external links takes you to services with their own privacy practices. Loading externally hosted resources can also disclose ordinary connection information to their providers.

7. Retention and security

Inquiry records may remain in form-submission records and email correspondence while an inquiry is handled and for related follow-up, recordkeeping, or legal needs. Provider logs and analytics have their own retention settings. There is no single deletion period covering all of these records. Contact us to ask about information relating to you or to request deletion.

No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Avoid sending sensitive information through the general contact form. If you believe information has been disclosed improperly, contact us using the email below.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You can browse public reading materials without submitting the contact form. Depending on applicable law, you may request access to your personal information, correction, erasure or blocking, object to processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Some requests may be subject to legal exceptions. We may need proportionate information to verify that a request concerns your own records.

Email leuneib01@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request,” identifying the interaction and your request. Please do not send identity documents unless a necessary verification method has first been agreed. You may also contact the Philippine National Privacy Commission about your data privacy rights or concerns.

9. Children’s privacy

Public resources can be read without submitting personal information. Parents and guardians should supervise children’s use of contact forms and external services. If you believe a child has submitted personal information that should be removed, contact us so the records can be reviewed and the appropriate action taken.

10. Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be published on this page with a revised date. For questions about this policy or the handling of your information, contact CrownMind Institute at leuneib01@gmail.com or visit our Contact page.
