Behind Enemy Lines Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

This policy applies to the Behind Enemy Lines Android app (net.crownmind.behindenemylines) from CrownMind Institute.

Information handled by the app

The app displays its introduction from files included with the installation. It does not require an account or ask for personal information. It does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools. CrownMind Institute does not collect, sell, or share personal information through the app.

Book, audiobook, and website links

The app opens the book and audiobook hosted by GitHub and the CrownMind Institute website in your device’s browser. When you follow a link, your browser connects to the destination. The destination may receive information such as your IP address and browser details. Any cookies, logs, or other information handled by an external service are governed by that service’s privacy practices.

  • GitHub General Privacy Statement
  • CrownMind Institute website

Retention and deletion

The app has no user accounts or personal records for CrownMind Institute to retain or delete. You can manage browser history and cookies in your browser settings and remove downloaded copies of the book or audiobook from your device.

If you email us, we use your email address and the information you provide to respond. We keep correspondence as long as needed to handle your request or meet applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to request deletion of your correspondence.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email CrownMind Institute at contact@crownmind.net.
