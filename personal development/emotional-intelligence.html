A CLOSER LOOK

About this book

Presented in the manuscript as Emotional Intelligence, Lived, this book develops four central domains of emotional intelligence and explores their practical application. It considers relationships, family life, work, ethical responsibility, and the daily discipline of becoming more emotionally fluent.

Bring emotional intelligence into everyday life through self-awareness, self-management, social awareness, and relationship skills. A reflective guide to applying these capacities at home, at work, and through adversity.

Key themes

  • The four domains of emotional intelligence
  • Relationships, work, and family life
  • Ethics and daily emotional practice
