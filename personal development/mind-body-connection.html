About this book

The Mind–Body Connection: Personal Development and Mental Health examines how mental and physical experience relate within everyday life. Its chapters discuss awareness practices, nutrition and movement, emotional intelligence, resilience, and navigating mental health challenges from an educational perspective.

Explore the relationship between inner experience, bodily awareness, and daily habits. This book brings together reflection on personal development, emotional regulation, movement, and lifestyle.

Key themes

  • Mind–body awareness and daily practices
  • Movement, nutrition, and lifestyle
  • Emotional regulation and resilience
