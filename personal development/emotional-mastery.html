A CLOSER LOOK

About this book

Emotional Mastery treats feelings as experiences to understand rather than simply suppress. Its progression moves from the nature and purpose of emotions to identifying triggers, reframing perspectives, and applying emotional understanding in relationships, resilience, and leadership.

Explore how to recognize, name, and respond to emotions with greater care. This guide connects emotional awareness with perspective, self-regulation, empathy, and values-led action.

Key themes

  • Recognizing triggers and naming emotions
  • Regulation, perspective, and presence
  • Empathy and alignment with values
