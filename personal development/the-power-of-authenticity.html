A CLOSER LOOK

About this book

This book presents authenticity as alignment between values and action. It explores identity, the cost of inauthentic living, self-doubt, fear, self-compassion, and the courage to build honest relationships. Later sections connect authenticity with communication, work, purpose, and daily life.

Examine the distance between outward expectations and inward values. Explore self-awareness, vulnerability, courage, and the practice of expressing yourself with greater honesty and integrity.

Key themes

  • Values, identity, and inner honesty
  • Vulnerability and self-compassion
  • Authentic relationships and purposeful work
