A CLOSER LOOK

About this book

The Art Of Effective Communication invites readers to examine not only how they speak, but how they connect. It explores sending and receiving messages, perception, attention, and the obstacles that interfere with understanding, encouraging practice in real conversations.

Explore communication as a practice of understanding and connection. Reflect on the barriers that distort meaning, the role of context, and the habits that support clearer, more thoughtful conversations.

Key themes

  • Clarity in sending and receiving messages
  • Perception, context, and communication barriers
  • Listening and intentional connection
