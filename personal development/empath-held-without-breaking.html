A CLOSER LOOK

About this book

Held Without Breaking explores the challenges of feeling deeply without losing a sense of self. Its chapters address emotional overload, saying no, navigating conflict, self-care, and bringing empathic strengths into work and relationships. The emphasis is on sustainable care and thoughtful limits.

A grounded exploration of empathy, sensitivity, and healthy boundaries. Reflect on caring deeply while protecting your capacity, recognizing emotional overload, and building more reciprocal relationships.

Key themes

  • Sensitivity and emotional overload
  • Boundaries and saying no without guilt
  • Reciprocity, self-care, and meaningful work
