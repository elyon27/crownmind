Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026 · Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Personal Development Books website or Android app, explore the collection, follow a book link, or contact the author.

1. About this website

Personal Development Books showcases nine books by Bienuel C. Tenio. In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Bienuel C. Tenio as the operator of this book showcase. This policy covers these website pages and the Personal Development Books Android app (package net.crownmind.personaldevelopmentbooks); third-party services and websites have their own policies.

For privacy questions or requests, contact bctenio27@proton.me.

2. Information collected through these pages

The website pages do not contain account registration, contact forms, newsletter subscriptions, comments, or an on-site checkout. They do not ask you to submit your name, email address, payment details, or health information to browse the books.

The current website code does not include analytics scripts, advertising pixels, tracking cookies, or browser local-storage features. Book covers and styles are served with the website rather than loaded from an external advertising or analytics service.

Android app

The Android app bundles the book showcase pages and cover images for offline browsing. It does not include an account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or in-app checkout, and requests no Android permissions. The app does not send browsing activity or personal information to the author. Gumroad, CrownMind Institute, and email links open external applications, where the destination service’s privacy practices apply. The app contains book summaries rather than the purchased full books.

3. Hosting, technical information, and cookies

When you access the hosted website, its hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information such as your IP address, browser or device information, requested pages, request times, and security logs to deliver and protect the service. Provider practices and retention periods are governed by their own policies and service settings.

This Privacy Policy is published on crownmind.net and is intended to be accessible without signing in. Separately hosted private versions of the book showcase may require sign-in through their hosting platform. That platform may use authentication or security cookies and process account information separately from these book showcase pages. The absence of tracking code in the pages does not mean that the hosting platform processes no information or uses no cookies.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking necessary platform cookies may affect access to the private hosted version.

4. Messages you choose to send

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any information or attachments you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request, provide relevant support, and maintain necessary correspondence. Please do not send passwords, full payment card details, or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Opening an email link uses your email application or provider. Email delivery and storage are also subject to the providers involved.

5. Gumroad purchases

Each “Get the book on Gumroad” link takes you to an external Gumroad product page. Payments and delivery take place through Gumroad, not through this website’s pages. Gumroad may collect contact, transaction, and payment-related information under its own policies. Review Gumroad’s Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab) before purchasing.

As the book seller, we may receive buyer and order information made available by Gumroad, such as an email address and purchase details. Such information may be used to fulfill and support purchases, resolve order inquiries, and maintain required transaction records. This showcase does not collect or store payment card details.

6. CrownMind Institute and other external links

The CrownMind Institute header link opens crownmind.net (opens in a new tab). Its information handling is outside the scope of this policy. Review the policies of any external website you visit. A link does not extend this policy to that destination.

Our external links use browser protections that suppress the referring page information and prevent the destination from controlling this page through the new tab.

7. Use and disclosure of information

Information received in connection with inquiries or purchases is used for the purposes described above. We do not sell or rent personal information received through these activities. Information may be handled by service providers needed for hosting, email, and transactions, or disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to address fraud, security issues, or legal claims.

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their privacy policies describe the arrangements applicable to their services.

8. Retention and security

We retain correspondence and purchase-related information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond to requests, provide support, maintain necessary records, or meet applicable legal obligations. The appropriate period depends on the type of record and the reason it is held. Hosting, email, and payment providers apply their own retention practices.

We use reasonable precautions for information under our control. No internet transmission, email service, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Your privacy choices and requests

You can browse the book pages without sending us a message or making a purchase. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, object to or limit certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on it.

Send requests concerning information we control to bctenio27@proton.me. We may need proportionate information to confirm your identity and understand the request. Some records may need to be retained to comply with legal obligations or resolve an outstanding transaction. Requests about information controlled by Gumroad or a hosting provider should also be directed to that provider. You may contact the relevant privacy regulator if you believe your concerns have not been addressed.

10. Children’s privacy

This website is a general book showcase and does not include features designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information that should be removed, contact us so we can review the request.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when website features or information-handling practices change. The revised policy will appear on this page with an updated date. Material changes will be explained here or through another appropriate notice.
