Transformation Houses

Sacred places of remembrance, prayer, and rest.

The envisioned Transformation Houses are private burial and resting sanctuaries for departed immediate family members, relatives, and friends. They are not public cemeteries, residential formation centers, schools, or a separate church.

Their purpose is to provide dignified places for the care of earthly remains and the prayerful remembrance of loved ones who have died. In the Catholic Christian faith guiding this intention, we entrust the departed to God's mercy and do not claim to determine their spiritual destiny.

Most of CrownMind Institute's Bible study, prayer, community, and educational resources are offered freely. Proceeds from selected paid books and publications are intended to support the future development of these sanctuaries. The Transformation Houses remain a proposed mission, not an established public facility.
